# Worked Example — Website Redesign Project (fictional)

This is a fictional scenario used to illustrate Stages 01-04. No real client, company, or project is depicted.

## Stage 01 — Discovery
A fictional 12-person consulting firm, "Kestrel Advisory," has a website last updated four years ago. It no longer reflects their current service lines, and two prospective clients have mentioned it looked outdated during sales calls.

## Stage 02 — Objectives
- The new site accurately represents Kestrel's current 4 service lines within 3 months.
- At least one prospective client per month mentions the site positively during a sales call (a soft, observational signal — not a hard KPI to build the whole project around).
- The site loads in under 2 seconds on mobile.

## Stage 03 — Stakeholders
| Name/Role | Level |
|---|---|
| Managing Partner | Decide |
| Head of Marketing | Decide (day-to-day) |
| 2 Senior Consultants | Consulted (service line accuracy) |
| All staff | Informed |

## Stage 04 — Scope (using `TEMPLATES/project-scope-template.md`)

**In scope:** 6 pages (Home, 4 Service Lines, Contact), English only, content migration from the old site, mobile-responsive design.

**Out of scope:** Blog/news section, multi-language support, integration with their CRM.

**Definition of done (excerpt):** "All 6 pages live on the production domain, reviewed and approved in writing by the Head of Marketing, passing a mobile responsiveness check on 3 real devices."
