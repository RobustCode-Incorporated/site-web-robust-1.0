## The 13-stage system

01. Project Discovery
02. Objectives
03. Stakeholders
04. Scope
05. Requirements
06. Deliverables
07. Dependencies
08. Risks
09. Planning
10. Execution
11. Monitoring
12. Validation
13. Closure

Each stage in the full system pairs a short explanation of *why* the stage matters with a template to fill in and, where an AI assistant genuinely helps, a structured prompt to draft a first version for you to review.
