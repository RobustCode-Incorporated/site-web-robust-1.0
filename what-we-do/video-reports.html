The problem

Great work disappears into slides

Monthly reports are long, written for the author, and rarely read to the end. Managers, partners and clients want three answers: where are we, can we trust it, and what happens next. A short narrated film answers them in minutes, on any phone.

How it works

Set up once. Ready every month.

01

Connect your data

We connect to the sources that already hold the truth: project tools, tests and deployments, CRM or business figures.

02

Your voice, with consent

We clone the voice of your spokesperson with their recorded consent, or design a voice for you. It runs locally and is always labelled as AI.

03

Your brand, once

Colours, fonts, logo and tone are designed into the animation once, so every report looks like you.

04

Every month, it's ready

A new film is generated from the latest evidence. You review the script, approve it, and share it.

What you get

A report people watch to the end

  • A monthly narrated video report built only from verifiable evidence: lifecycle stage, proof, next milestone.
  • Formats for every channel: 16:9 for meetings and LinkedIn, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16 for social feeds and Reels.
  • Burned-in captions, so it works with the sound off.
  • A human-approved script every time. Nothing is published without your review.
  • Transparent AI: synthetic narration is always disclosed on screen.

Questions

Is it really our spokesperson's voice?

Yes, if they want it to be. We only clone a voice with the recorded consent of the person it belongs to. If you prefer, we design a voice instead.

Where is our data processed?

Voice generation runs locally on our machines, not on a public voice platform. Data access is agreed with you during setup, in line with GDPR.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your data sources, languages and how often you report. Tell us what you need and we will send you a clear quote.
